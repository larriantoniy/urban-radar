# Content draft human review V1

Use this template for each `content-draft-v1`. Scores are deliberately left
blank: content generation never self-approves a draft.

| Source item | Fact fidelity (1–5) | Naturalness (1–5) | Interest (1–5) | Brand consistency (1–5) | Reviewer notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `source/source_item_id` |  |  |  |  |  |

Scoring guide:

- **Fact fidelity**: 5 means every claim is supported by the supplied source
  and editorial evidence; 1 means material invention or distortion.
- **Naturalness**: 5 reads like an ordinary local-city post; 1 is plainly
  robotic or bureaucratic.
- **Interest**: 5 invites a resident to keep reading; 1 is a dull document
  retelling.
- **Brand consistency**: 5 fits “Тольятти меняется”; 1 sounds like another
  kind of media outlet.

Approval, editing, and any later publication remain separate human and
publisher workflows.
